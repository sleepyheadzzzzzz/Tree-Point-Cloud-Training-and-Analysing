# Figure caption

**Figure 7. Mapped environmental change from 2015–2017 to 2021–2023.**
Change is calculated as the later-period value minus the earlier-period value
for (A) surrounding-tree density within 25 m, (B) monoculture rate, (C)
sky-view factor, (D) solar radiation, (E) land-surface temperature, and (F)
daytime noise. Because period-specific noise data were available for 2017 and
2022, panel F shows the 2022 minus 2017 change in the maximum mapped daytime
noise class across road, railway, tram, and metro sources; locations outside
the mapped noise contours were assigned the 40 dB quiet-condition value used
in the spatial diagnosis. Panels A and C use red for negative changes and green
for positive changes. In panels B, D, E, and F, the colour direction is
reversed: green denotes negative changes and red positive changes. Pale colours
indicate little or no change. To retain local spatial detail while reducing the
influence of extreme cells, each panel uses its own symmetric colour limits
defined by the 99th percentile of the absolute change. The maps describe
spatial change in the available indicators and should not be interpreted as
causal environmental effects.

## Short caption

**Figure 7. Environmental change between the early and late monitoring
periods.** Panels A–F show later-minus-earlier changes in tree density,
monoculture rate, sky-view factor, solar radiation, land-surface temperature,
and daytime noise, respectively. Panels A and C use red for negative and green
for positive changes; panels B, D, E, and F use the reversed direction, with
green for negative and red for positive changes. Pale colours indicate little
or no change. Panel F compares the available 2017 and 2022 daytime-noise
layers; all panels use variable-specific symmetric 99th-percentile colour
limits.
